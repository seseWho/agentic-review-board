# Feature Specification: Critic v0.1

**Branch**: `005-critic`  
**Date**: 2026-08-22  
**Status**: Draft — awaiting review and explicit approval before planning

## 1. Purpose

Slice 5 introduces the Critic as the fourth executable participant in the Agentic Review Board MVP.

The Critic SHALL challenge the quality and support of the review artifacts produced so far without taking ownership of final ranking or recommendation.

It answers:

> Where are the weak assumptions, contradictions, omissions, unsupported conclusions, underestimated risks, or otherwise insufficiently supported points in the current review?

The Critic is adversarial toward weak reasoning, not adversarial for its own sake.

This slice extends the already implemented execution sequence:

```text
ReviewInput
    ↓
Analyst
    ↓ ProblemAnalysis
Solution Architect
    ↓ SolutionAlternatives
Risk Reviewer
    ↓ RiskAssessment
Critic
    ↓ CriticalReview
Coordinator (still pending / not implemented in Slice 5)
```

Slice 5 SHALL preserve all accepted Slice 1–4 contracts unless an explicit approved change is required.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In Scope

Slice 5 SHALL add the minimum behavior required to:

1. represent a validated Critic output as an explicit `CriticalReview` contract;
2. allow the Critic to consume exactly the current review's:
   - original `ReviewInput`;
   - validated `ProblemAnalysis`;
   - validated `SolutionAlternatives`;
   - validated `RiskAssessment`;
3. challenge weak or unsupported upstream reasoning using visible review artifacts;
4. identify contradictions when supported by current review artifacts;
5. identify genuinely material missing alternative classes without designing a replacement solution portfolio;
6. explicitly validate points that appear sufficiently supported;
7. preserve unresolved issues and newly identified uncertainty for downstream use;
8. extend `StageExecution`, `Review`, and the explicit `ReviewOrchestrator` sequence through Critic;
9. fail closed when the Critic produces invalid structured output or invalid references;
10. preserve exact same-execution provenance for the Critic stage;
11. leave Coordinator unimplemented and `PENDING` after successful Slice 5 execution.

### 3.2 Out of Scope

Slice 5 SHALL NOT introduce:

- Coordinator implementation;
- final ranking of alternatives;
- preferred alternative selection;
- final recommendation;
- `FinalReview` production;
- autonomous decisions or external actions;
- retries, timeout policy, concurrency, asynchronous orchestration, or distributed execution;
- persistence, RAG, document ingestion, vector databases, web browsing, tools, MCP, email, Teams, or other integrations;
- provider-specific SDK dependencies in the domain/application core;
- organization-specific policy knowledge not present in the current review;
- a generic workflow engine or stage registry;
- a generic cross-stage artifact-reference framework unless separately justified and approved;
- automatic rewriting or mutation of Analyst, Solution Architect, or Risk Reviewer outputs;
- a replacement solution portfolio generated by Critic;
- numeric confidence/probability scoring without a separately approved methodology.

## 4. User Stories

### US1 — Produce an evidence-based critical review

As a user, I want the Critic to inspect the review artifacts produced so far and return concise structured challenges so that weak reasoning is visible before any final recommendation is produced.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. Critic receives the original `ReviewInput`, `ProblemAnalysis`, `SolutionAlternatives`, and `RiskAssessment` from the same current review execution.
2. Critic returns a validated `CriticalReview` rather than unstructured free-form text alone.
3. Material criticisms identify what is being challenged whenever the current review artifacts make such identification possible.
4. Criticism is grounded in visible current-review artifacts rather than fabricated facts or external hidden information.
5. The Critic does not rank alternatives or recommend a winner.

---

### US2 — Preserve explicit traceability of criticism

As a user, I want material criticisms to point back to explicit review artifacts so that I can inspect why a challenge exists.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. A material criticism identifies its target type.
2. A material criticism references the explicit target item whenever practical and unambiguous.
3. The Critic can challenge assumptions, alternatives or their characteristics, risks or mitigations, constraint interpretation, and cross-stage conclusions when supported by the current review.
4. Invalid, unknown, mismatched, or ambiguous generated references fail closed rather than being silently repaired.
5. Generated references are validated against the explicit current Critic inputs and are not confused with StageExecution same-execution provenance.

---

### US3 — Allow agreement as well as challenge

As a user, I want the Critic to be able to state that earlier points are adequately supported so that the system does not manufacture disagreement merely to make the multi-agent interaction look active.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. `CriticalReview` can explicitly contain validated/supported points.
2. The Critic is not required to manufacture at least one criticism.
3. A valid review with no material criticism remains representable.
4. A validated point does not become a final recommendation or ranking.
5. The Critic does not silently upgrade an assumption into a fact by validating it.

---

### US4 — Surface missing alternatives and contradictions without rewriting upstream work

As a user, I want the Critic to expose omissions and contradictions without replacing prior artifacts so that the Coordinator receives both the original analysis and the challenge to it.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. Critic may identify a genuinely material missing alternative class.
2. A missing-alternative finding explains what category appears omitted and why that omission matters.
3. Critic does not fully design or insert a replacement `SolutionAlternative` into `SolutionAlternatives`.
4. Critic may identify contradictions between explicit artifacts, including facts vs assumptions, assumptions vs constraints, alternative claims vs risks, advantages vs disadvantages, or other upstream conclusions.
5. Upstream validated artifacts remain unchanged.
6. Newly identified uncertainty or unresolved issues remain explicit.

---

### US5 — Execute Critic as the fourth mandatory stage

As a user, I want Critic to run only after Analyst, Solution Architect, and Risk Reviewer have succeeded so that critical review is based on the complete validated upstream evidence available at this point.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. Critic starts only when Analyst, Solution Architect, and Risk Reviewer are `COMPLETED` with valid outputs.
2. The Critic stage records the exact current ReviewInput, ProblemAnalysis, SolutionAlternatives, and RiskAssessment instances used for that execution.
3. A successful Critic stage becomes `COMPLETED` with a validated `CriticalReview`.
4. After successful Critic completion, Review remains `RUNNING` because Coordinator is still `PENDING` in Slice 5.
5. Critic failure makes the Review `INCOMPLETE` and leaves Coordinator `PENDING`.
6. Completed upstream results remain inspectable after Critic failure.
7. Critic cannot execute if any mandatory upstream stage failed or has not completed.

---

### US6 — Fail closed at the Critic trust boundary

As a user, I want malformed or unsupported Critic output to stop the review before Coordinator so that invalid criticism cannot silently become trusted decision-support evidence.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. Raw model output is untrusted until structurally validated.
2. Contextual references are validated before the `CriticalReview` becomes trusted application state.
3. Structural or contextual generated-output failure produces a dedicated Critic output-validation failure.
4. Ordinary participant/model execution failure is distinguishable from output-validation failure.
5. No partially trusted `CriticalReview` is stored when any required part of the output fails validation.
6. Coordinator remains unexecuted after Critic failure.
7. Internal domain/invariant failures outside the participant-call boundary are not silently converted into participant failures.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### CRIT-FR-001 — Explicit Critic Inputs

The Critic SHALL receive:

```text
ReviewInput
ProblemAnalysis
SolutionAlternatives
RiskAssessment
```

from the current review execution.

### CRIT-FR-002 — Structured CriticalReview Output

The Critic SHALL produce a structured `CriticalReview` containing the conceptual information defined by the canonical agent model:

```text
CriticalReview
- criticisms[]
- validated_points[]
- missing_alternatives[]
- contradictions[]
- unresolved_issues[]
```

The exact implementation structure of these collections SHALL be refined in the plan/data-model stage and SHALL remain consistent with the canonical semantics.

### CRIT-FR-003 — Structured Criticism

Each material criticism SHOULD support the canonical conceptual fields:

```text
Criticism
- id
- target_type
- target_reference
- issue
- importance
- rationale
- correction_or_question?
```

The exact representation of `target_type` and `target_reference` is intentionally deferred to the data model.

### CRIT-FR-004 — Qualitative Importance

Criticism importance MAY use the existing qualitative vocabulary:

```text
LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
```

If used, it SHALL remain an ordinal review judgment and SHALL NOT be represented as a calibrated probability or numeric score.

### CRIT-FR-005 — Evidence-Constrained Criticism

Material Critic findings SHALL be grounded in the explicit current review artifacts.

The Critic SHALL NOT introduce fabricated user facts or organization-specific policies not supplied by the review.

### CRIT-FR-006 — Preserve Facts and Assumptions

The Critic SHALL preserve the semantic distinction between facts and assumptions.

Repetition, validation, or challenge by Critic SHALL NOT promote an assumption to fact unless the original ReviewInput explicitly supports that statement as a fact.

### CRIT-FR-007 — No Artificial Disagreement

The Critic MUST NOT invent objections merely to create conflict.

A valid `CriticalReview` MAY therefore contain no material criticisms when the current artifacts do not justify one.

### CRIT-FR-008 — Validated Points

The Critic SHALL be able to identify upstream points that appear adequately supported.

Such validation indicates that a point survived the Critic's review; it does not make that point a final decision or Coordinator recommendation.

### CRIT-FR-009 — Missing Alternatives

Critic MAY identify a genuinely material omitted alternative class.

For Slice 5 it SHALL describe the omission and why it matters, but SHALL NOT mutate `SolutionAlternatives` or construct a replacement solution set.

### CRIT-FR-010 — Contradictions

Critic SHALL be able to represent contradictions between explicit current-review artifacts when present.

Contradiction reporting SHALL preserve both sides of the contradiction sufficiently for downstream inspection.

The exact structured representation SHALL be defined in the data model rather than guessed in this specification.

### CRIT-FR-011 — Unresolved Issues

Critic SHALL preserve material unresolved issues or newly identified uncertainty in explicit output fields rather than silently resolving them with invented information.

### CRIT-FR-012 — No Ranking or Recommendation

Critic SHALL NOT:

- rank alternatives;
- select a preferred alternative;
- declare a winner;
- issue the final recommendation;
- present criticism importance as a substitute ranking of alternatives.

Those responsibilities remain with Coordinator.

### CRIT-FR-013 — No Silent Upstream Mutation

Critic SHALL NOT rewrite, replace, or mutate:

- `ProblemAnalysis`;
- `SolutionAlternatives`;
- `RiskAssessment`.

Its output SHALL coexist with the upstream artifacts as a new review artifact.

### CRIT-FR-014 — Structured Validation Boundary

Generated Critic output SHALL be treated as untrusted until validated against the approved structured contract.

Invalid output SHALL NOT enter trusted Review state or propagate to Coordinator.

### CRIT-FR-015 — Current-Review Contextual Validation

Where Critic output references explicit upstream artifacts, the references SHALL resolve against the exact current Critic inputs before the output is trusted.

No-match or ambiguous references SHALL fail closed.

### CRIT-FR-016 — Same-Execution Provenance

A non-PENDING Critic `StageExecution` SHALL retain provenance to the exact current ReviewInput and exact completed outputs of Analyst, Solution Architect, and Risk Reviewer used by that Critic execution.

Equal-but-independent copies SHALL not satisfy this provenance requirement.

### CRIT-FR-017 — Critic Lifecycle

Slice 5 SHALL support the Critic stage states:

```text
PENDING
RUNNING
COMPLETED
FAILED
```

with the same immutable validated lifecycle semantics already established for implemented stages.

### CRIT-FR-018 — Coordinator Remains Pending

Coordinator SHALL remain `PENDING` throughout Slice 5.

Therefore successful Slice 5 execution SHALL not create `ReviewStatus.COMPLETED` or a `FinalReview`.

### CRIT-FR-019 — Failure Semantics

If Critic fails:

1. Critic becomes `FAILED`;
2. Review becomes `INCOMPLETE`;
3. Coordinator remains `PENDING`;
4. validated Analyst, Solution Architect, and Risk Reviewer outputs remain preserved and inspectable;
5. no Critic output is trusted or propagated.

### CRIT-FR-020 — Review Isolation

Critic implementation SHALL retain no hidden mutable review state between executions.

A later fresh Review SHALL not inherit criticisms, validated points, contradictions, missing alternatives, unresolved issues, or other Critic artifacts from a prior Review.

## 6. Architectural and Quality Constraints

### CRIT-NFR-001 — Provider Independence

Critic domain/application semantics SHALL NOT depend on a concrete provider SDK.

### CRIT-NFR-002 — Deterministic Testability

All Slice 5 core behavior SHALL be testable with deterministic model substitutes and without a live network or LLM service.

### CRIT-NFR-003 — Explicit Orchestration

Slice 5 SHALL extend the existing explicit synchronous sequence only as far as necessary to invoke Critic after successful Risk Reviewer completion.

A generic stage loop, registry, graph engine, or orchestration framework SHALL NOT be introduced solely for this slice.

### CRIT-NFR-004 — Minimal New Abstractions

The implementation SHALL prefer Critic-specific contracts and reuse existing trusted concepts where they semantically fit.

A generic cross-stage reference framework SHALL NOT be introduced merely because Critic needs references to multiple upstream artifact types.

### CRIT-NFR-005 — Immutable Trusted Contracts

New trusted Critic contracts SHALL follow the accepted structured-contract approach already used by the project, including closed schemas and immutable validated objects where applicable.

### CRIT-NFR-006 — Bounded Output

Critic output SHOULD remain concise and inspectable, favoring structured findings over essays or hidden reasoning.

### CRIT-NFR-007 — No Private Chain-of-Thought Dependency

Critic traceability SHALL rely only on explicit structured findings, rationale, targets, and current review artifacts.

The product SHALL NOT require or expose private chain-of-thought reasoning.

### CRIT-NFR-008 — No Persistence Requirement

Slice 5 SHALL remain in-memory and SHALL NOT require persistent review storage.

## 7. Required Slice 5 End States

### 7.1 Successful Slice 5 execution

The successful observable state SHALL be:

```text
Review                 RUNNING
Analyst                COMPLETED
Solution Architect     COMPLETED
Risk Reviewer          COMPLETED
Critic                 COMPLETED
Coordinator            PENDING
```

A `CriticalReview` is available as the trusted Critic stage output.

No final ranking or recommendation exists yet.

### 7.2 Critic failure

The required failure state SHALL be:

```text
Review                 INCOMPLETE
Analyst                COMPLETED
Solution Architect     COMPLETED
Risk Reviewer          COMPLETED
Critic                 FAILED
Coordinator            PENDING
```

Previously completed stage outputs remain inspectable.

### 7.3 Upstream failure

If Analyst, Solution Architect, or Risk Reviewer has failed, Critic SHALL remain `PENDING` and SHALL NOT execute.

## 8. Edge Cases That Must Remain Valid

The implementation and later test plan SHALL account for at least the following cases:

1. Critic finds no material criticism and returns only validated points or an otherwise valid empty critical result if the final approved data model permits it.
2. Critic challenges an explicit Analyst assumption without turning it into a fact.
3. Critic validates a strong point rather than objecting artificially.
4. Critic identifies an omitted alternative class without generating a replacement alternative.
5. Critic identifies a contradiction spanning two different upstream artifact types.
6. Critic references a current Risk and its mitigation.
7. Critic-generated reference points to an unknown upstream item.
8. Critic-generated text reference is ambiguous within the relevant current collection.
9. Solution Architect produced a valid zero-alternative result and Critic still evaluates the resulting review rather than fabricating alternatives.
10. Risk Reviewer produced a valid zero-risk assessment and Critic still evaluates whether other weaknesses exist without fabricating risks.
11. Structurally malformed Critic output.
12. Ordinary model/provider failure during Critic invocation.
13. Internal invariant failure outside the participant boundary.
14. Reusing the same Critic/orchestrator objects across multiple Review executions without cross-review contamination.
15. Noncanonical but semantically valid stage tuple ordering continues to be interpreted by `StageIdentity`, not positional index.
